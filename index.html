Features & Mechanics

🔄 Inverted KeyMap

Pressing Enter deletes text, and pressing Backspace adds a new line. It completely messes with your brain while you try to type normally!

⏳ Inactivity Timer

If you stop typing for 5 seconds, your text starts disappearing letter by letter. You have to keep typing quickly to save your document!

🎨 Dynamic Colorize

Every word you type gets a random bright color. It makes the screen look chaotic, vibrant, and incredibly fun to look at.
